Our Services

Complete dental care, under one roof.

From routine cleanings to advanced cosmetic and restorative treatments — every service delivered with precision and warmth.

01

General Dentistry

Routine check-ups, consultations, fillings, and preventive care to keep your teeth healthy for life.

Includes oral exams, tooth-coloured fillings, fluoride care and personalized prevention plans for the whole family.

02

Pedodontics

Dentistry for children — specialist care from the very first milk tooth to healthy teenage smiles, delivered gently and fear-free.

Fear-free first visits, cavity prevention, fluoride and sealants, and child-friendly fillings delivered at your child’s pace.

03

Scaling

Professional scaling and polishing that removes plaque, tartar and stains — leaving your smile fresh.

Ultrasonic scaling plus stain-busting polish, with tips to keep gums healthy and breath fresh between visits.

04

Periodontal

Treatment of the gums and the bone that support your teeth — for bleeding, swollen or receding gums, before teeth loosen.

Deep cleaning (root planing), gum infection control and maintenance plans to stop gum disease from progressing.

05

Root Canal

Painless, precision root canal treatment that saves your natural tooth and relieves deep discomfort.

Single-sitting RCT available with rotary endodontics and digital length measurement for maximum comfort.

06

Minor Oral Surgeries

Safe, comfortable minor procedures — tooth extractions, wisdom teeth and small soft-tissue surgeries.

Performed under local anaesthesia with careful after-care guidance, so healing is quick and worry-free.

07

Dental Implants

Permanent, natural-looking tooth replacement using modern implant systems designed to last decades.

Guided implant placement with premium titanium systems — eat, speak and smile with total confidence.

08

Braces & Aligners

Traditional braces and clear aligners to straighten teeth, correct bites and transform your smile.

Metal, ceramic and clear-aligner options with digital progress tracking at every appointment.

09

Cosmetic Dentistry

Smile design, whitening, veneers and aesthetic refinements tailored to your face and personality.

Digital smile previews, professional whitening and minimal-prep veneers crafted for a natural finish.

Dental care in Karaikudi: common questions

Dr. Mani’s Multispeciality Dental Center provides general and specialist dental care in Sekkalai, Karaikudi. Find practical answers about appointments, treatments and visiting the clinic below.

Where is Dr. Mani’s Dental Center in Karaikudi?

Dr. Mani’s Multispeciality Dental Center is at No. 315/24, MAM Building, opposite Jeyakumaran Furniture, Sekkalai, Karaikudi – 630001, Tamil Nadu. On-site parking is available.

What are the clinic’s opening hours?

The clinic is open Monday to Saturday from 9:30 AM to 9:00 PM, India Standard Time. Sunday visits are by appointment only. Call +91 95516 24126 to arrange your visit.

How do I book a dental appointment?

Call +91 95516 24126 or complete the appointment form on this page. The form prepares a WhatsApp message; tap Send in WhatsApp to send your request. The clinic will confirm your appointment. Walk-ins are welcome, but appointments are preferred.

Which dental treatments are available?

The clinic offers general dentistry, children’s dentistry, scaling, periodontal care, root canal treatment, minor oral surgery, dental implants, braces and aligners, and cosmetic dentistry. A consultation helps determine the appropriate treatment.

Does the clinic treat children?

Yes. The clinic offers children’s dentistry, including first visits, cavity prevention, fluoride care, sealants and fillings. Dr. S. Saraswathy Meena, BDS, MDS, is the clinic’s pediatric dentist.

Is an X-ray included with the first consultation?

The clinic offers a free X-ray with the first consultation. Contact the clinic for consultation fees and details before your visit.
